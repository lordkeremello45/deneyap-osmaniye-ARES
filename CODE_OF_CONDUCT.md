# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ARES contributors aim to make collaboration respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, professional, and constructive.
- Critique technical ideas, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Accept good-faith corrections and review feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information without permission.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and its safety, reliability, and accessibility goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and persistent disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method on their public GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other project-managed channels.

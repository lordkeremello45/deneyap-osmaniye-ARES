ARES is developed by the **ROBO-TECH** team as a search-and-rescue UAV research and prototyping project.

These roles describe the team's current areas of responsibility and do not imply that each person authored every component. Component-level contributions should be credited in relevant source files, commits, or release notes.

## Open-source contributions

GitHub automatically records code contributions in the repository's [contributors graph](https://github.com/lordkeremello45/deneyap-osmaniye-ARES/graphs/contributors). The graph may not include every kind of contribution, such as documentation, testing, hardware experiments, issue triage, or design work.

When adding a contributor here, confirm the preferred public name and the person's consent. Do not publish private contact details.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for contribution guidelines.
